# Grill Me

Interview me relentlessly about every aspect of this plan until we reach a shared understanding. Walk down each branch of the design tree, resolving dependencies between decisions one by one.

- Ask ONE question at a time. Wait for my answer before the next one.
- Give your recommended answer with every question.
- Resolve a decision before you ask about the decisions that depend on it.
- If the codebase can answer a question, explore the codebase instead of asking.
- For safety-related projects, include failure modes, safe states and recovery in the branches you walk.
- Stop when every branch is resolved. Then list the decisions we made, one line each.
